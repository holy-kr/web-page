What we worked on

In Week 2 we dealt with circuit components and practical work. We were told to develop our projects, and we worked on two ideas that connected electronics with movement and control.

  • Voltage is the electrical pressure that pushes current through a circuit.
  • Current is the flow of electric charge, measured in amperes.
  • Resistance limits current and is measured in ohms.
  • We practiced series vs. parallel connections and wiring LEDs with resistors on the breadboard.
  • Project 1: a Bluetooth-controlled car that was meant to be controlled by a smartphone.
  • Project 2: a joystick-controlled car.

Tools we needed and their uses:

  • Breadboard for building and testing circuits without soldering.
  • Jumper wires for connecting different components together.
  • LEDs to test whether the circuit was working properly.
  • Resistors to limit current and protect the components.
  • Arduino board to control the circuit and run the project.
  • Battery pack or power supply to provide power to the circuit.
